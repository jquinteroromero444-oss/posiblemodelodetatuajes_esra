## Brand & Style
The brand embodies an artisanal, mystic, and deeply personal tattoo and fine-line aesthetic rooted in Granada. It merges dark neo-Japanese iconography, botanical organic silhouettes, and delicate Andalusian ornamentation with subtle, sharp motifs of spiders, threads, and faceted obsidian crystals. The design style blends dark-mode editorial luxury with tactile, ceremonial refinement: velvety carbon tones, deep crimson punctures, and antique gilded highlights.

The interface should evoke intimacy, master craftsmanship, and timeless reverence. Interfaces must feel quiet, spacious, and gallery-focused, allowing high-resolution flash art, body anatomy placements, and intricate microblading lines to command absolute attention without utilitarian clutter.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an abyssal dark spectrum that shifts from graphite obsidian (`#0f0f11`) to raised velvet slate (`#17171a` and `#1f1f24`). The chromatic primary is a ceremonial carmine red (`#b91c1c`), used deliberately for definitive actions, active states, and visceral focal points. 

Secondary and tertiary hues are weathered golds (`#c5a880` and `#d4af37`), evoking Japanese kintsugi and Spanish altarpiece gilding; these are reserved for delicate hairline borders, badges, category micro-labels, and subtle accents. Typography and iconography rely on soft antique ivory (`#f3ede2`) and muted parchment gray (`#a6a096`) rather than sterile pure white, preserving nocturnal warmth and high-contrast readability without eye strain.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm pairs the dramatic, literary serifs of Playfair Display with the clean, highly legible precision of Plus Jakarta Sans. 

Editorial titles and section headers employ Playfair Display to establish a romantic, occult, and artisanal aura, occasionally applying italic styling to evocative pull-quotes or artist bios. Functional text, forms, technical consultation sheets, and portfolio metadata rely strictly on Plus Jakarta Sans, maintaining crisp rendering even against deep dark backgrounds. Overlines and micro-tags utilize all-caps tracking with expanded letter-spacing to emulate fine jewelry marks and luxury atelier numbering.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around open, breathing compositions reminiscent of fine art catalogues. Desktop views apply generous section padding and gutters to elevate visual weight, while mobile devices compress margins to preserve canvas for high-detail imagery.

Grid alignments honor asymmetrical rhythm: portfolio pieces may span offset columns (e.g., 7 columns for imagery paired with 5 columns for narrative copy) to avoid sterile algorithmic uniformity. Content strictly separates visual art modules with ample vertical margins (`space-xl` and above) to prevent detail fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy relies on stacked carbon surface containers paired with hairline antique metallic edges rather than heavy, blurry dropshadows. 

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#0f0f11` ground with an optional faint, fine-grain film texture.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#17171a` framed by a 1px continuous border of `border-gold-subtle`.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Drawers, Modals):** `#1f1f24` surrounded by a soft golden ambient halo (`0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.35)`).
- **Interactive Depth:** Hover states on interactive cards do not lift via physical extrusion; instead, they trigger a delicate inner border brightening (`#c5a880`) and a faint, carmine ember glow emanating behind the element.

## Shapes
The shape system uses a soft, restrained rounding level (`1`). Radius measures 4px (`0.25rem`) for base elements and buttons, expanding to 8px (`0.5rem`) on primary modal enclosures and portfolio tiles. 

This restrained geometry preserves the crisp, faceted precision of cut crystal and needlework, intentionally avoiding over-rounded, juvenile pills. Decorative containers may feature beveled or notched micro-corners (chamfers) on specialized banners or feature cards to subtly echo Japanese crests and crystal prisms.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Carmine Impact):** Rich crimson background (`#b91c1c`), ivory text (`#f3ede2`), 4px corner radius, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. On hover, transitions to a luminous scarlet with a soft crimson glow (`carmine-glow`).
- **Secondary Action (Gilded Outline):** Transparent background, 1px border in antique gold (`#c5a880`), text in `#f3ede2`. On hover, fills with semi-transparent gold (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.1)`) and border shifts to `#d4af37`.
- **Ghost/Text Action:** Understated ivory label with a thin gold hairline underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact tags for tattoo styles (e.g., "Fine Line", "Micro-realismo", "Irezumi", "Microblading").
- Surface of `#1f1f24`, text in muted ivory (`#a6a096`), enclosed in a 1px border of `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.25)`. Active chips shift text to ivory and border to full antique gold.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Background: `#17171a`. Border: 1px subtle muted tone (`rgba(243, 237, 226, 0.15)`).
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans body font in `#f3ede2` with placeholders in `#a6a096`.
- Focus State: Border transitions instantly to antique gold (`#c5a880`) with an inset box shadow of `0 0 0 1px #c5a880`. Error states shift border to carmine red (`#b91c1c`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with `#17171a` background and 1px gold border.
- Selected state fills with solid carmine red (`#b91c1c`), displaying an ivory check or dot icon.

### Cards & Flash Art Displays
- Surface of `#17171a` with 1px border in `border-gold-subtle`.
- Image containers feature a slight inner shadow vignette to blend seamlessly into the dark UI. Captions display Playfair Display titles followed by Plus Jakarta Sans micro-details.

### Specialized Studio Components
- **Artist Availability Pill:** An obsidian badge housing a pulsing crimson or gold light indicating open calendar books.
- **Before & After Comparative Slider:** Microblading treatment comparisons bounded by antique gold divider handles with hairline vertical indicators.
- **Healing & Care Accordion:** Sleek horizontal rule dividers containing collapsed healing timelines, highlighted by gold plus/minus iconography.
